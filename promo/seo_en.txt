[Game Title]
Duck Shoot Carnival

[Game Introduction]
Duck Shoot Carnival brings the classic carnival shooting gallery to your browser! Cheery ducks parade across the fairground booth — grab your trusty shotgun, take aim and knock those wacky ducks right off the rails. Rendered in a charming Western cartoon-farm art style: plump 3D cartoon ducks, bright macaron colors, and a living carnival scene with spinning windmills, a ferris wheel, waving bunting, drifting clouds and passing birds — all animated in real time. 30 levels each with a unique gameplay twist, 3 epic bosses, and an unlockable Endless mode. Chain hits for score multipliers, bag golden ducks for triple points, and finish flawless for 3 stars. 100% free, no download, plays great with mouse & keyboard or touch.

[How to Play]
1. Aim & Shoot: move the mouse (desktop) or press & drag (mobile) to steer the crosshair; click or press Space (desktop), quick tap (mobile) to fire.
2. Limited Ammo: only 3 shells per wave — watch an ad for more, or wait for auto-reload between waves.
3. Lives: every escaped duck costs one heart (4 in total). Lose them all and it's game over — revive by watching an ad.
4. Scoring: 100 base points per hit plus a time bonus for quick shots; consecutive hits build a combo multiplier up to x5; golden ducks are worth 3x.
5. Level Mods: every level plays differently — twin ducks, gusty wind, night spotlight, balloon ducks, thick fog, mirror controls, armored ducks, thunderstorm, golden rush and more; meet a unique boss every 10 levels (Baron Von Plume / Major Duckington / DJ Quacker) with armored plating and weak-spot gimmicks.
6. Stars: finish a level with zero escapes for 3 stars (2 stars for <=2 escapes). Collect stars to unlock Endless mode with randomized mod combos.
